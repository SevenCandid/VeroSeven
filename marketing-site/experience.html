The Evolution

We are not just hosting events. We are building a continuous pipeline of talent—from sparking interest in competitions, to deep training in our academy, and finally integrating top minds into the tech industry.

Phase 1: Spark Curiosity

Launch of the VeroSeven Digital Challenge to identify talent and inspire innovation.

Phase 2: Foster Skills

Introduction of Holiday Tech Camps and School Tech Clubs for foundational training.

Phase 3: Formal Education

Establishment of the VeroSeven Academy for rigorous, industry-standard software engineering curriculum.

Phase 4: Industry Integration

The Mentorship Network and direct integration into VeroSeven Labs or partner tech firms.
